## Design Context

### Users
BingeSync is for two people who are trying to decide what to eat together with less back-and-forth. They are usually on their phones, moving quickly, and want to get from "what should we eat?" to a confident shared pick in under a couple of minutes.

### Brand Personality
Playful, social, decisive. The product should feel lightweight and optimistic, with enough personality to make the session feel shared rather than transactional.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a bright, food-centric interface with warm accents, confident typography, and celebratory result states. Prioritize a clean light theme with rich surfaces, expressive emoji cues, and motion that makes the workflow feel smooth instead of static.

### Design Principles
- Make the next action obvious at every step.
- Treat the top-ranked result like a clear winner, not just another row.
- Keep collaboration visible through shared-session cues, join-code handling, and participant states.
- Design for mobile first, with touch-friendly controls and readable hierarchy.
- Use motion to acknowledge actions and smooth transitions, never as decoration alone.
