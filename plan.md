**App Idea: Shared Show Picker for Couples**

**Problem**
Couples often struggle to decide what show or movie to watch together. Each person has different tastes, and scrolling through streaming services can take longer than actually watching something.

**Concept**
Create an app where two people (or couples) input the shows they are interested in watching. The app then helps identify which shows both people would enjoy.

**How It Works**

1. **Create a Shared Session**
   - Two users join the same session (for example, two partners in a couple).

2. **Add Shows**
   - Each person adds shows they want to watch to a shared pool.
   - This creates a compiled list containing all suggested shows.

3. **Rating Quiz**
   - Each person goes through the list and rates each show from **1–10** based on how willing they are to watch it.

4. **Compatibility Calculation**
   - The app compares both users’ ratings.
   - Shows with strong ratings from both people are ranked higher.

5. **Mutual Recommendation List**
   - The final output is a **ranked list of shows both people are most likely to enjoy together**.

**Key Features**

- Simple rating system (1–10)
- Shared show list
- Algorithm that highlights mutual interest
- Ranked recommendation results
- Possibly connect with streaming platforms to show where the show is available

**Value**

- Removes decision fatigue.
- Helps couples quickly find something both people will enjoy.
- Turns choosing a show into a quick, collaborative process instead of an argument.

**Potential Future Features**

- Swipe-style rating (like/dislike).
- Group mode (more than two people).
- Integration with Netflix, Disney+, etc.
- AI suggestions based on both users’ tastes.

**Core Idea in One Sentence**
An app that helps couples decide what to watch by combining their show lists and using ratings to generate a ranked list of mutually appealing options.
